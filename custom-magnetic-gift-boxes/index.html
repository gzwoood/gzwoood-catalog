Magnetic Gift Boxes for Brands That Care About Unboxing

Magnetic gift boxes are one of the most popular packaging choices for brands that want a premium unboxing experience without overcomplicating the structure. The hidden magnetic closure gives the box a clean opening feel, while the rigid cardboard body protects the product and creates a high-value retail presentation.

At GZ Woood Packaging, we manufacture custom magnetic gift boxes for beauty, jewelry, fashion, fragrance, electronics, corporate gifts and luxury retail brands. Each box can be customized by size, paper material, logo, color, insert, handle, ribbon and surface finishing.

Export Cartons

Interior protection

Custom Inserts for Better Protection and Presentation

For magnetic gift boxes, the insert is often just as important as the outer box. A well-designed insert keeps the product secure during shipping and makes the unboxing moment feel more professional.

  • EVA foam inserts for perfume, electronics, tools and sample kits.
  • Velvet inserts for jewelry, watches, fragrance and premium gift sets.
  • Paperboard inserts for cosmetics, candles and recyclable retail kits.
  • Satin lining for apparel, scarves, jewelry and high-end gifts.
  • Molded pulp inserts for brands looking to reduce plastic-based materials.
Printing and finishing

Finishing Options That Shape the Brand Feel

Surface finishing can completely change how a magnetic gift box feels. For premium packaging, we usually recommend combining one main paper material with one or two finishing techniques instead of overloading the design.

Gold / Silver Foil Stamping

A premium metallic logo effect for luxury, jewelry, cosmetics and gift packaging.

Embossing / Debossing

Creates a raised or recessed logo texture for a subtle and tactile brand detail.

Spot UV Coating

Adds gloss to selected areas such as logos, patterns or product names.

Soft-Touch Lamination

Creates a smooth matte surface with a premium hand feel.

Full CMYK Printing

Suitable for colorful artwork, campaign packaging and brand-heavy designs.

Pantone Color Matching

Helps keep brand colors more consistent across production batches.
