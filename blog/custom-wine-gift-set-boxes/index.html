Custom Wine Gift Set Boxes: Packaging That Makes the Gift Feel Complete

Design protective, premium packaging for wine bottles, glasses and accessories—made for retail, corporate gifting and seasonal campaigns.

Published July 31, 2026 · GZ Woood Packaging

A good bottle of wine is already a thoughtful gift. The right packaging makes it feel more complete. Whether the set is intended for a wedding, corporate event, holiday promotion or retail collection, the box is the first thing the recipient sees.

It should protect the bottle, keep every item in place and present the set in a way that feels carefully considered. For wine brands and gift companies, custom packaging also creates more space to communicate the story behind the product.

More Than a Box for a Bottle

A standard wine box is usually designed to hold one bottle. A wine gift set box often needs to do more. It may include glasses, a corkscrew, stopper, opener, tasting card or other accessories. Each item should have its own position so the presentation remains neat when the lid is opened.

This matters especially for gift sets sold online or shipped internationally. A properly designed insert keeps the entire set secure and organised during transport.

Choosing the Right Box Structure

The box structure should depend on bottle size, accessory count and how the set will be sold. Rigid boxes are commonly used for premium wine sets because they provide good support and a substantial feel.

  • Magnetic closure boxes
  • Hinged-lid presentation boxes
  • Lift-off lid boxes
  • Drawer-style boxes
  • Two-door presentation boxes

For a set containing one bottle and two glasses, a book-style magnetic box is a practical choice. It displays all products clearly while the rigid construction supports the weight of the glass bottle. The best structure is not always the most complicated one: it should be easy to open, strong enough for the product and right for the intended selling price.

A Secure Insert Is Essential

Wine bottles and glasses have very different shapes, so the insert needs careful planning. EVA foam is often used for premium gift sets because it can be cut accurately for the bottle, stemware and accessories. It offers firm support and can be covered with paper, fabric or velvet-like material.

Paperboard inserts are an option for brands seeking a more recyclable solution, and molded pulp may suit bottle-only sets or simpler designs. Exact product dimensions, photos and physical samples help ensure a secure fit—just a few millimetres can make a meaningful difference.

Finishing Details That Suit Wine Packaging

The material, colour and logo treatment usually have the greatest impact. Dark blue, black, burgundy and deep green work well for premium wine gift boxes, while natural kraft and textured paper suit more traditional or organic presentations.

  • Gold or silver foil stamping
  • Embossed or debossed logos
  • Matte or soft-touch lamination
  • Textured speciality paper
  • Printed inner panels, ribbon pulls or fabric handles

Gold foil works particularly well on dark-coloured boxes when used with restraint. The inside can also carry the winery story, tasting notes or a message for the recipient.

Packaging for Corporate and Seasonal Gifting

Wine gift sets are frequently used for Christmas, New Year, anniversaries, customer appreciation and employee gifts. Corporate orders may need to display both the wine brand and gifting company logo through foil stamping, printed sleeves, cards or removable labels.

Plan seasonal packaging early: custom inserts, special paper and foil stamping all require production time before major holiday periods.

Consider Shipping From the Beginning

Wine gift boxes can become heavy, particularly with a full bottle and two glasses. Design the outer presentation box together with the shipping carton. Protective corner pieces, foam support and stronger corrugated cartons may be needed for international delivery.

The bottle should not press directly against the outer wall. For e-commerce, an additional mailer around the rigid gift box can protect decorative surfaces from scratches, dirt and shipping labels.

Sustainability Without Losing the Premium Feel

FSC-certified paper, recyclable paperboard inserts and right-sized packaging can reduce unnecessary material use and shipping volume. A well-constructed rigid box with responsibly sourced paper and a simple finish can still feel high-end.

Information Needed Before Customising a Wine Gift Box

  • Bottle dimensions and weight
  • Photos of the bottle, glasses and accessories
  • Preferred box style and order quantity
  • Logo or artwork files
  • Preferred colours and finishes
  • Delivery country and target date

For sets with multiple products, a simple layout showing where each item should sit saves time during design. A physical sample is strongly recommended before mass production.
