Custom Magnetic Gift Boxes: MOQ, Cost, Materials & Production Guide

A practical guide for brands comparing magnetic rigid box structures, materials, inserts, order quantities and production options.

Published November 20, 2026

Custom magnetic gift boxes are rigid presentation boxes with concealed magnets built into the lid and front wall. They are widely used for cosmetics, perfume, apparel, jewellery, spirits, seasonal gifts and premium product sets because the structure combines protection with a controlled opening experience.

This guide explains the decisions that affect appearance, unit cost and delivery: box structure, packed size, board thickness, wrapping paper, insert, printing, finishing, order quantity and shipping method. Our standard production MOQ is 200 pieces per custom size and design. Selected structures may support a 100-piece trial run at a higher unit cost, while 500 pieces or more normally offers better value.

Materials That Determine the Box Quality

Most magnetic rigid boxes use greyboard as the structural core, wrapped with printed art paper, specialty paper, textured paper or fabric. Board thickness should suit the box dimensions and product weight. An oversized box with insufficient board thickness can flex, while excessive thickness increases material and freight cost without always improving performance.

  • Greyboard: provides rigidity and structural strength.
  • Art paper: supports full-colour printing and lamination.
  • Specialty or textured paper: creates a more tactile presentation with restrained branding.
  • Fabric wrap: suits premium keepsake packaging but requires careful colour and glue control.
  • FSC-certified options: may be available depending on the selected paper and project specification.

MOQ applies per custom size and design. Multiple colours, sizes or artworks may be treated as separate production specifications. Very large boxes, special papers and complex inserts can require a higher minimum.

What Affects the Unit Price?

There is no reliable single price for a custom magnetic box without a specification. The main cost drivers are finished dimensions, board thickness, wrapping material, print coverage, finishing, insert complexity, magnets, order quantity and packing method.

  • Larger dimensions increase board, paper and shipping volume.
  • Foil stamping, embossing, Spot UV and special lamination add production processes.
  • Custom-cut foam and multi-level inserts require more material and labour.
  • Foldable structures may reduce freight volume but add assembly components.
  • Higher quantities spread setup costs over more units.

Printing and Finishing Options

Common choices include offset printing, Pantone colour matching, foil stamping, embossing, debossing, Spot UV, matte lamination, soft-touch lamination and textured paper. A premium result does not require every finish. One controlled logo treatment on a well-proportioned box often looks more refined than combining too many decorative processes.

Assembled or Flat-Pack Shipping?

Classic magnetic rigid boxes are delivered assembled and ready to pack, but they occupy more shipping and storage volume. Foldable magnetic boxes ship flat and are assembled before product packing, which can improve logistics efficiency for large formats and international orders. The right choice depends on labour availability, box size, quantity and required presentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for custom magnetic gift boxes?

Our standard MOQ is 200 pieces per custom size and design. Selected structures may support a 100-piece trial run at a higher unit price, while 500 pieces or more generally provides better value.

How much does a custom magnetic box cost?

Pricing depends on dimensions, board thickness, wrapping paper, print coverage, finishing, insert, quantity and packing method. Packed product size and quantity are required for a useful quotation.

Can I order a sample before mass production?

Yes. A physical sample is recommended to verify size, magnet alignment, colour, finishing and insert fit. Sampling normally takes about 3–5 working days after specifications are confirmed.

How long does production take?

Typical production is approximately 12–18 working days after sample approval. Complex finishes, special materials and larger quantities may require more time.

Can magnetic rigid boxes be shipped flat?

Foldable magnetic structures can ship flat. Traditional hinged rigid boxes are normally delivered assembled. Flat-pack designs can reduce storage and international freight volume.

Which insert is best for perfume or cosmetics?

EVA foam provides accurate positioning and strong protection. Paperboard inserts suit lighter products and a paper-based appearance, while satin-covered inserts are used when presentation is the priority.

Can the box use FSC-certified paper?

FSC-certified paper options may be available depending on the selected paper, colour and project specification. Certification scope should be confirmed before quotation and production.
