Clear scope

What the documents mean

They can support

  • Buyer material due diligence
  • Technical documentation for a selected substrate
  • Project compliance discussions before production
  • Traceable evidence from participating suppliers

They do not automatically prove

  • That every GZ Woood product is “PPWR certified”
  • That one sample represents every supplier or specification
  • That a raw-material result certifies a finished package
  • That the document replaces buyer-specific legal review
Buyer questions

Frequently asked

Are all GZ Woood products PPWR certified?

No. PPWR is a regulatory framework, not a single universal product certificate. We provide applicable material evidence and project documentation based on the selected specification.

Can I receive the complete test report?

Yes. Qualified buyers may request complete applicable reports for project evaluation. Commercially sensitive supplier information may be shared under appropriate confidentiality arrangements.

Can you prepare documents for my specific order?

We first confirm the material, supplier route, end use and destination market, then identify which available reports are relevant and whether additional testing is needed.

Need documents for buyer review?

Send us your product type, packaging structure, destination market and required standard. We will confirm which evidence is available for your project.
