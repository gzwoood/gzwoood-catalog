Handle options

Compare real handle constructions

The handle attachment affects appearance, carrying comfort, load capacity and production cost. We recommend the structure after reviewing the bag size and packed weight.

01

Buckle Fastening

A visible decorative fixing that adds a distinctive premium detail.

02

Knotted Rope Handle

A classic secure construction for cotton or twisted rope handles.

03

Embedded Ribbon or Cord

The handle end is concealed for a cleaner exterior presentation.

04

Glued Reinforcement Card

An internal paper card helps secure the handle and distribute carrying load.

05

Die-Cut Handle

An integrated hand opening creates a minimal look without a separate cord.

Recommended approach: ribbon or embedded cord for premium apparel bags; knotted rope for stronger everyday carrying; die-cut handles for a clean, integrated structure.

Buyer questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ?

The usual MOQ for this style is 300 pieces. Exact requirements depend on size, paper, printing and handle selection.

Can the paper bag size be customized?

Yes. Width, side gusset and height can be developed around the product or inner packaging.

Which artwork files are preferred?

Vector AI, PDF or EPS artwork is preferred, together with Pantone references where color matching is important.

Can you add foil stamping or embossing?

Yes. Compatibility is confirmed after reviewing the paper, artwork size and desired effect.

What should I provide for an accurate quote?

Please send the finished size, quantity, packed weight, paper/handle preference, artwork, destination country and required delivery date.
