Buyer questions

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ?

The usual MOQ is 100 pieces. Exact requirements may vary with box size, paper, printing and finishing.

Can the size and insert be customized?

Yes. The box dimensions and insert layout are developed around the packed product.

How long does sampling take?

Most custom samples take approximately 3–5 working days after the specification and artwork are confirmed.

Which artwork files are preferred?

Vector AI, PDF or EPS files are preferred. High-resolution reference images can also help with the initial quotation.

Can you ship to the United States or Europe?

Yes. International delivery options are reviewed according to destination, quantity, carton volume and required timeline.
