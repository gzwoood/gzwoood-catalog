Structural planning

Built around product, packing and transit

The correct mailer is more than an outside dimension. We evaluate usable internal space, board thickness and how the product is loaded.

Information we need

  • Product sizeLength × width × height of the packed product.
  • Product weightSingle-unit weight and total packed weight.
  • Packing methodLoose, wrapped, bagged or held in an insert.
  • Shipping routeCourier, fulfillment center, retail delivery or gift presentation.

What we recommend

  • Internal sizeSpace allowance for loading, inserts and product protection.
  • Board gradePaper and flute combination appropriate for presentation and transit.
  • ClosureLocking tabs, tape, peel-and-seal or tear-strip configuration.
  • Insert routeCorrugated support, paperboard divider, foam or no insert.
Print & finish library

Build the right visual level

A real finish sample makes the differences easier to understand than abstract color blocks. Choose one process or combine compatible finishes after the corrugated paper and printing route are confirmed.

01

Foil Stamping

Metallic gold, silver or selected foil colors for logos and small premium graphic details.

02

Embossing

Raises selected artwork to create a tactile brand mark. Suitability depends on the paper and box construction.

03

Matte Finish

A low-reflection surface with a clean, understated presentation for premium mailer packaging.

04

Glossy Finish

A reflective surface that increases color depth and visual impact on selected printed papers.

05

Spot UV

Selective gloss applied to logos or patterns for controlled contrast against a matte background.

For corrugated mailer boxes: not every finish is suitable for every board grade. We will recommend a compatible paper wrap or printing route after reviewing your artwork, quantity and recyclability priorities.

Buyer questions

Frequently asked questions

What information is required for a quotation?

Please provide product or box dimensions, quantity, destination country, printing requirements and required delivery date.

Can the box include a tear strip?

Yes. A tear strip and peel-and-seal tape can be evaluated according to the box structure, paper and order quantity.

Can you print inside and outside?

Yes. Compatible inside and outside printing can be included after artwork and production requirements are reviewed.

Can you add an insert?

Yes. Corrugated supports, paperboard inserts and fitted foam are available depending on the product and protection level.
