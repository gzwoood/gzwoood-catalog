Materials and finishing

Custom Materials, Artwork and Surface Finishing

The cover is the most important part of a faux book box. We can tune the look from vintage and classic to clean, minimal and modern.

Rigid Cardboard

Common rigid greyboard thickness options include 1200 gsm, 1500 gsm, 1800 gsm and thicker board for larger boxes.

Fabric Wrapping

Cotton-linen fabric, textured paper, specialty paper, kraft paper and printed cover paper are available.

Foil Stamping

Gold, silver or custom foil stamping can highlight titles, logos, borders, patterns and decorative cover details.

Printing

Full CMYK printing, Pantone color matching and vintage-style artwork can be applied to the cover, spine and inside panels.

Magnetic Closure

Hidden magnets create a clean book-like opening feel and help keep the box closed during display and handling.

Custom Inserts

Paperboard, EVA foam, velvet, satin or molded pulp inserts can be added when the box needs to hold a product securely.

Buyer questions

Decorative Faux Book Boxes FAQ

What is the MOQ for custom faux book boxes?

Our usual MOQ for custom rigid faux book boxes is 100 pcs. Exact MOQ may vary by size, material, artwork and finishing requirements.

Can I customize the book cover artwork?

Yes. We can customize the front cover, back cover, spine, inside panels, logo, title, color and decorative patterns.

Can you make linen fabric faux book boxes?

Yes. Cotton-linen fabric wrapping is available, along with specialty paper, textured paper, kraft paper and printed paper options.

Can the faux book box have magnetic closure?

Yes. Hidden magnetic closure is available for book-style rigid boxes and helps create a clean, reusable opening feel.

How long does sampling take?

Most custom samples take around 3-5 working days after artwork, size and material details are confirmed.

What is the production time?

Standard production time is usually 12-18 working days after sample approval and deposit payment.

Can you add inserts inside the box?

Yes. We can add paperboard, EVA foam, velvet, satin or molded pulp inserts depending on the product and presentation needs.

Do you ship to the United States and Europe?

Yes. We support global delivery to 50+ countries, including the United States and European markets. DDP is available for selected countries.
